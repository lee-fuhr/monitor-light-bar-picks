How we choose our picks

We don’t run our own lab tests or timed trials. Instead, we spend weeks digging into owner reviews, forum discussions, and expert evaluations from established tech outlets. Our picks are based on real‑world reputation: what actually holds up after months of daily use, which models develop flicker, USB‑port failures, or loose clips, and which ones consistently receive praise for light quality and ease of use. We also track feedback from heavy users, remote workers, editors, and gamers, who rely on these bars for hours every day.
