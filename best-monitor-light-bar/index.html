A monitor light bar is one of the simplest upgrades you can make to your desk. Instead of a bulky desk lamp that eats up space and creates harsh shadows, a light bar sits right on top of your monitor and casts a wide, even pool of light across your keyboard and documents – without reflecting off your screen. That focused, adjustable light reduces eye strain in a dim room and makes late-night work or gaming far more comfortable. When choosing a light bar, the most important factors are light quality (color temperature, brightness, and uniformity), the control method (touch sensor, dial, or remote), and how it attaches to your monitor. Heavier bars with counterweights are more stable, while cheaper models may wobble. Some include a backlight that illuminates the wall behind your monitor for added ambience. Consider the thickness of your monitor bezel – many bars claim compatibility but may not fit ultra-thin panels or curved displays.
