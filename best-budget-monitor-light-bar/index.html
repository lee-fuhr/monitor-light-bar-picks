A monitor light bar is one of the simplest upgrades you can make to your workspace. It frees up desk space by clamping onto your monitor, casts a wide, even pool of light across your keyboard and notes, and, crucially, keeps glare off your screen. For anyone who works or games after dark, a good light bar can dramatically reduce eye strain and make your monitor look more contrasty, all for a fraction of the cost of a premium desk lamp. When shopping on a budget, the key trade-offs are build quality, light uniformity, and adjustability. Most sub- light bars now offer decent color temperature options (3000K–6500K), but you will encounter cheaper plastics, less smooth rotation, and sometimes a distracting central hot spot.
